1. Executive Summary & Vision
The Academic Intelligence Platform turns every test a student takes into personalised guidance. Rather than reporting a single mark, the platform identifies weak topics, explains why performance is low, and recommends exactly what the student should study next — all aligned to the student’s board and grade.
Vision
Build an Academic Intelligence Platform that helps:
Students improve marks with clear, daily, topic-level guidance.
Tutors know exactly what to teach and reteach next.
Parents clearly see improvement and receive early warnings.
Institute owners prove results, improve retention, and differentiate on admissions.
Core Value Proposition
Every test automatically identifies weak topics,
explains why performance is low, and recommends
exactly what the student should study next —
mapped to the correct Board and Grade.
This positioning is materially stronger than “Online Test Platform” or “AI Test Platform” because it speaks to the outcomes parents and institutes are willing to pay for.

3. Academic Data Foundation
This is the backbone of the platform. Without a disciplined academic hierarchy and complete question tagging, no meaningful analytics, diagnostics, readiness scores, or AI plans can be produced.
3.1 Academic Structure Hierarchy
Board and Grade are now first-class levels of the hierarchy — not optional fields. The same topic is taught differently across boards and grades, so analytics must be anchored to both.
Board → Grade → Subject → Chapter → Topic → Question
Illustrative hierarchy
CBSE
 └─ Grade 8
      └─ Mathematics
          └─ Algebra
              └─ Linear Equations
                  └─ Questions
 
State Board
 └─ Grade 8
      └─ Mathematics  (different syllabus / sequencing)
The same topic (e.g. Linear Equations) can appear at different grades with different difficulty, and across boards with different syllabus coverage. Anchoring to Board + Grade keeps every score comparable and curriculum-accurate.

3.4 Question Authoring & Bulk Excel Upload
Questions can be created individually, but the primary set-up path is a bulk Excel upload. Both Admin and Tutor roles can upload, so a tutor can rapidly build a question bank for their own batches without waiting on central admin.
The platform provides a downloadable Excel template whose columns map exactly to the mandatory question tags. Each row is one question: the tutor fills the sheet, uploads it, and the platform validates, previews, and then commits the questions to the bank.

Upload Workflow
Download the standard Excel template from the Question Bank screen.
Fill one question per row, completing all mandatory tag columns.
Upload the file; the platform validates every row.
Review the validation preview — valid rows are listed alongside any errors.
Fix the flagged rows, re-upload, then commit the valid questions to the bank.
Validation & Error Handling
Uploads are validated row-by-row, so a single bad row never blocks the rest of the file.
Every mandatory tag (Board, Grade, Subject, Chapter, Topic, Difficulty, Marks, Question Type) must be present on each row.
Board / Grade / Subject / Chapter / Topic must match the academic master; unmatched values are flagged for creation or correction.
Difficulty and Question Type must use allowed values, and Correct Answer must reference one of the supplied options.
Duplicate questions are detected within the file and against the existing bank.
Invalid rows are returned in a row-level error report; valid rows can still be committed immediately.
4. Platform Modules
Eleven modules form the functional core. Each is Board/Grade-aware: scores are computed and displayed against the student’s curriculum.
Module 1 — Assessment Engine
Delivers and captures the tests that feed every downstream analytic.
Features:  Online MCQ tests; subject-, chapter-, and topic-wise tests; Practice Mode and Assessment Mode; time tracking; question-attempt tracking.
Data captured:  Correct / incorrect, time taken, skipped questions, multiple attempts, and per-topic performance.
Module 2 — Student Diagnostic Engine
Moves beyond marks to expose actual learning gaps at topic level, framed by board and grade.
Instead of:  Score = 72%
 
CBSE Grade 8  ·  Mathematics
  Algebra      = 85%
  Geometry     = 40%
  Mensuration  = 35%
  Statistics   = 70%
Student insights:  strong topics, weak topics, accuracy %, speed analysis, and consistency analysis.
Module 3 — Academic Health Score
A single, understandable score (out of 100) built from accuracy, consistency, topic coverage and improvement trend.
Academic Health Score :  78 / 100
 
Strong Areas        Needs Attention
  Algebra             Geometry
  Arithmetic          Mensuration

Measures how ready a student is for upcoming school exams and for the next grade, against the specific board curriculum. Readiness is always shown with its board and grade context.

5. AI Features (The “Wow” Layer)
AI generates insight — it does not chat. Every AI output is grade- and board-aware and follows the board syllabus sequence.
AI 1 — Academic Diagnosis (Root Cause)
After every test, AI explains the cause behind a weak score, not just the score.
Geometry: understands formulas, but struggles
with application-based questions.
Likely cause   : weak visualisation.
Recommendation : 15 diagram-based questions.
AI 2 — Tutor Copilot (Grade + Board)
Auto-generates a class summary and the recommended next class, scoped to board and grade.
CBSE Grade 8  ·  Mathematics  ·  35 students
 
Strong : Algebra, Fractions
Weak   : Geometry, Mensuration
Next class: Mensuration Revision  (expected +8%)
AI 3 — Parent Explanation Engine
Translates technical analytics into plain language a parent can act on.
Your child can solve direct geometry questions
but struggles when multiple concepts are combined.
Focus on: Triangles, Angles, Area Problems.
AI 4 — Personalised Study Plan (Grade-Aware)
Builds a day-by-day plan from the student’s weak topics, target exam, available days, daily study time and target score — sequenced to the board syllabus.

         21 days · target 60% → 80%

  Priority: 1. Ratio  2. Linear Equations  3. Mensuration
  Day 1  Fractions Revision
  Day 2  Fractions Practice
  Day 3  Decimals Revision   ...
  Expected readiness: 82%

The plan updates itself as the student improves.

AI 6 — Readiness Prediction
Predicts an expected score range, always presented as an estimate.
